Research · McNair Scholars · 2026

Tracing a 30B vision model's LEGO puzzle failures

The problem

Qwen3-VL-30B gets about 23% on LEGO-Puzzles, a benchmark of spatial reasoning tasks. It's a mixture-of-experts model, so I wanted to find out if a few specific experts or layers caused the failures.

What I did

I profiled which experts the router picks across all 48 layers, ranked the layers by z-score, and clustered the expert "fingerprints" with k-medoids. Then I ran a 40-experiment ablation study on rented A100s through RunPod.

What I learned

My best ablation took test accuracy from 23% to 26.5%. I checked it with McNemar's test and a held-out set, and the gain didn't hold up. The errors come from all over the model, so there's no small set of layers to fix. I wrote it up as a negative result.

Python · Qwen3-VL · RunPod · NumPy · Pandas · scikit-learn

Berkeley Lab · Summer 2023

Precipitation classifier on a Raspberry Pi

The problem: a mmWave radar spits out raw binary files. I needed to classify the precipitation on a device small enough to leave out in the field.

What I did: I wrote a decoder that turns the binaries into clean CSVs using JSON header configs, then trained SVM and K-means classifiers that hit 90%+ accuracy across conditions. I deployed it on a Raspberry Pi, where it classifies a reading in under a second.

Python · Linux · NumPy · Raspberry Pi
